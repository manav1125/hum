# Cue work surfaces v6 — the arc in, the arc out, and the hygiene

One self-contained file: **cue-work-surfaces-v6.html**. 7 frames + 2 spec blocks. v1–v5 designed the product for someone who already understands it and whose Cue behaves; v6 covers everything else.

## A · The three that decide retention
- **A1 · Day one HQ** — no tour. One question ("what's the biggest thing you're trying to get done this quarter?") produces the first mission without using the word first; three cards then explain mission → watching → hand-off in experience order, not schema order. **Setup meter is non-shaming by construction**: 5 steps, 2 pre-done, "no rush — Cue works either way", and the last two steps are the converting moments (first hand-off, first weekly review) not profile busywork. Disappears permanently at step 5.
- **A2 · The correction** — *it worked and it shouldn't have* (not a failed tool call). **Five rules, in order:** (1) Cue reports itself, always before the user finds out, even at 3am; (2) show the artefact verbatim; (3) own it in the first person ("I chased her anyway"); (4) bring a drafted fix the user may decline; (5) narrow its own authority, scoped to the failure class, with the root cause offered as the real fix. Pushes the rest of the deck down; always breaks quiet hours; lands in the act ledger and the weekly "acts reversed" count. **Red is reserved for this.**
- **A3 · Interruption budget** — written as promises about *volume*, not per-event toggles: four tiers (right-now-always / within-the-hour, max 2 a day / morning brief / never), only tier 1 breaks quiet hours, and a chart making the counter-intuitive point concrete — **19 interruptions/week on Assist vs 3 on Autonomous**. Trust isn't a risk you take, it's how you get your attention back.

## B · Second tier
- **B1 · Hand off to a human** — people are just another recipient in the existing Y2 sheet. Two honest distinctions: whether they have Cue (rich hand-off vs plain email Cue then tracks), and **the item never leaves your mission** (you keep the ring, "waiting on Jess" reuses Y3). Visibility line is explicit and narrow: this item + its thread, nothing else, never Life.
- **B2 · Continuity** (spec, no new screen) — server-side triage cursor with resume banner · drafts follow with take-over not fork · voice requests surface as "from your voice note, 8:12" · never two live copies · shared read state across devices.
- **B3 · Reasoning panel** — provenance for *judgement*, not filing. Every line is a decision + a **clickable real source** (charter, brand voice, a specific memory, the trust setting). No model-internals, no confidence percentages. Key row: *"I didn't send it — that's your current trust setting, not a limitation of mine."* "Correct me" edits **the rule, not the draft**.

## C · Pre-ship hygiene
- **C1 · Accessibility** (ship blockers) — never colour alone (the glyph-first taxonomy is the answer) · rings carry text labels, and metric-less rings announce state not numbers · deck is keyboard-complete (J/K, ↵, seven single-key verbs, ⌘K, Esc) · live regions rate-limited to 20s on the focused region only · motion is decoration with static equivalents under prefers-reduced-motion · **4.5:1 minimum in both themes, with the passing muted tokens named explicitly: light `#6B6B60` (5.4:1 on white) — `#8A8A7E` (3.1:1) and `#A8A89C` (2.4:1) are ground/hairline colours only and must never carry text; dark `#9A9AA8` — never `#5B5B68` for text.** All v6 frames were corrected to these values, so the frames and the spec now agree.
- **C2 · Your data** — export everything / **export work-only** (the Life lens makes handovers trivial) / **forget a person** / auto-forget raw data with memories persisting · close = agents stop immediately, 30 days to export, then real deletion, **no reactivation nagging, no exit-survey wall** · "Pause everything instead" as a genuine equal.
- **C3 · Bulk recovery** — "I filed 41 things wrong": counts by destination, and the number that defuses it — **"anything sent or spent — none."** One button undoes all 41, the offending rule is already paused, root cause offered as a fix. Detected by unusual destination share in a window; Cue reports rather than waits to be caught (same rule as A2).

## Schema cost
Setup-progress record · `act_correction` type (bad act + correction + leash change) feeding the existing reversed-act count · interruption log to enforce the ceiling · `assignee_type: agent | human` · reasoning trace per generated artefact · server-side triage cursor + draft locks · filing-run grouping so 41 acts undo as one.
