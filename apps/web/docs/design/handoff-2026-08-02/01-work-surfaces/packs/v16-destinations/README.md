# v16 — the three destinations (2026-08-02)

HQ, Work and All conversations at full size in the v15 shell, so the rail's three-item peek and its destination can be judged together.

**The test each screen had to pass:** the peek must feel like a *preview* of this, not a substitute for it.

## D1 · HQ — earns the click with *why*
Rail gives three titles and a time. HQ adds **the thing each belongs to, the draft state, the blocking count, the calendar fit** — everything needed to decide — plus the day rail (with the named free block), the delivered row, and the four Tier-3 lines (arrivals / waiting / pulse).

## D2 · Work — earns it with *the counts*
Things view. A ring alone is a dashboard tile; **"1 needs you · 2 running · 9 total" with the agents named** is a door you can see a room behind. Sub-nav carries the three views (Things · Everything · Files) with the Professional/Personal filter beside it. **Personal appears as one row under Ongoing**, opening to its horizons — not a separate tab.

## D3 · All conversations — earns it with *quotes*
Two things make this better than a title list:
1. **A one-line quote from the thread** — people find old conversations by remembering a sentence, not a title they never wrote.
2. **The ▤ thing chip** — the conversation and the work it belongs to stay visibly connected.

Filters are by thing, plus **"Unattached · 12"** — which doubles as the honest count of chats that never became work.

## Note
Nothing here is new design — all three follow v10–v12 as already specified. This pass draws them in the v15 shell for sign-off.
