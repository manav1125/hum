# v7 — the volume answer and the first screen (2026-08-02)

Answers the two judgement calls in `BRIEF-2026-08-02-volume-and-first-screen.md`, plus the connector-gate frame and the task-row question.

## The headline finding
**It was never the item count that overwhelmed — it was twenty-six cards of chrome around eleven items.** At 8–12 items HQ is not heavy once the frame stops competing with the content. The fix is mostly subtraction, and it holds at 30 items too, because the parts that grow are the two lists, not the frame.

## The tension, resolved — §A three tiers (RETIRES "always render")
"Always render" conflated two things. Honesty requires a lane never be *silently absent* and never fake a number. It does **not** require a card.

- **Tier 1 — always a card:** Needs you · Delivered · Missions. Even at zero, because "nothing needs you" deserves the space.
- **Tier 2 — a card only when non-empty:** In motion · Life · day rail · batch offer · correction. When empty they demote to Tier 3, never to nothing.
- **Tier 3 — one grey line, always present:** Arrivals · Waiting · Rhythms · Pulse, plus demoted Tier 2. Glyph + full sentence + link. **This is where honest empty states now live.** "Nothing has arrived — because nothing is watching" is a Tier 3 *line*, not a card.

`Never a fake number` and `a no-op is not a success` survive untouched.

## Frames
- **P1 · Landing (the new default surface)** — mark, greeting, **one delivery sentence**, composer, three prompts, one hint. **The sentence IS the door** — no companion "show me" button, because two affordances for one intent is the clutter we're removing. Suggested prompts are drawn from real state (a needs-you item, the free block, an active mission) — that's how a clean surface still proves Cue knows things. Rail collapses to icons; the needs-you count is the only badge in the app.
- **P2 · HQ at 11 items** — eleven cards, down from twenty-six. **Needs-you comes first here**, deliberately reversing canonical order, because you arrived via a sentence that already delivered the receipts. Four Tier-3 lines above the census carry arrivals, waiting, rhythms and pulse — including "0 need you", a real number, not a hidden absence.
- **P3 · The stack row** — the shape that replaces a hundred rows: **stacks** (one sender identity, many notices — HSBC's three addresses are one stack), **threads** (one conversation → one task), **dropped** (with the rule in Cue's words, always inspectable). Critical: a stack containing anything actionable says **"1 may matter"** on the row — collapsing may never bury a live item.
- **P4 · Connector gate** — a soft gate, not a wall. The **can't/can list** turns a demand into an explanation; the escape hatch stays but is honestly labelled a lesser product ("use Cue as a chat assistant"). **"Read-only to start"** removes the real objection, which is never effort — it's handing your inbox to something that might send from it.

## §B · The row — three slots, provenance behind
1. **The verb phrase** — "Reply to Rachel about the NDA redlines". Never a subject line, never a sender name.
2. **The mission chip** — `◎ Renew Acme`, the permanent breadcrumb.
3. **The one thing that decides *when*** — exactly one, by priority: hard deadline > blocking count > calendar fit > age.

Behind the row (hover tooltip / detail block): sender, arrival time, channel, why Cue filed it here, confidence, ✨ Move. **Provenance answers a question most rows are never asked** — putting it on the line is what made 103 rows unreadable. *Exception:* low filing confidence shows a `?` instead of a mission chip — that's a request, not provenance.

## Rules added
- The app **opens on the composer**; HQ is a destination (click the sentence or ⌘1).
- **Suggested prompts must come from real state** — never generic starters.
- **Stacks group by sender identity, not address**; actionable content must surface on the stack row.
- **Dropped items keep their reason in Cue's words** and stay one click from view — a filter you can't inspect is a filter you can't trust.

## Precedence
v7 supersedes canonical K1's *render policy* and the landing surface. K1's nine-row **order**, the eight verbs, Life-as-lens, and all invariants stand. Apply the three tiers to K1 rather than redrawing it.
