# v19 — navigation rethink (2026-08-02) · supersedes v18 §1 and v15's CUE group

## The fault line I missed
My "CUE" group treated **Skills** and **People** as the same kind of thing because they answered similar-sounding questions. They aren't:

- **People and Library accumulate** — they get richer every week whether or not you touch them.
- **Skills, Memory, Watching, Tools, Marketplace, Rhythms, Agents, Identity, Trust are configuration** — you go, change one thing, leave.

**Demoting People to a Memory tab was the worst of it.** Memories about a person *inform* People; they aren't the interface to it. The interface is browsing by person — a different job.

## People (frame N2) — never drawn before
A **relationship surface, not a contact list**. Three columns:
1. **Who** — name, org, role
2. **What Cue has learned** — habits, preferences, positions, patterns, in **plain sentences not fields** ("Replies before 10am, never after 6. Prefers email over Slack for anything contractual.")
3. **Relationship state** — *you owe a reply · waiting on her · **going quiet***

Filters: All · In touch this month · **Owe them a reply** · **Going quiet** · By company.

**"Going quiet · 7" is the one nothing else has.** A CRM tells you who exists; this tells you who's slipping away. Row click → their timeline: every exchange, what was agreed, files sent, open items both sides.

Footer states the honest thing: *"Cue learns these from your channels — nothing here was typed in by hand."*

## Your Cue (frame N3) — eleven surfaces, one destination
Left-hand tabs, grouped by the question each answers:

| Group | Tabs |
|---|---|
| **Who works for you** | Agents · Skills · Tools & apps · Marketplace |
| **What it knows & does** | Memory · Rhythms · Watching |
| **You & trust** | Identity · Trust · Preferences |

**Agents stays the first tab** — richest surface, and the one people show others. Every tier chip, agent chip and pulse line in the app **deep-links straight to the relevant tab**, so the common path never goes through this door.

## The rule, restated
- **Does it get richer on its own?** → destination.
- **Do you only go there to change something?** → tab in Your Cue.

**Why this beats my earlier "does it demo well"** — demo value is a judgement call and it drifted. *Does the data accumulate* is a property you can check.

**Adding a twelfth config surface costs a tab. Adding a sixth destination requires proving its data accumulates.**
