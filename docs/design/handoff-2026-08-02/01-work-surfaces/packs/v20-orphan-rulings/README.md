## People — hold the promotion. Agreed, for your reason.
A destination with two rows teaches people the slot is worthless, and that lesson is hard to un-teach.

- **Interim:** ship People as a tab inside **Your Cue → Memory**.
- **Promotion criterion, stated so it's checkable:** People earns the sidebar row when contact memories are **non-zero and growing week over week** — not when the code ships.
- **One design ask while it's broken:** the People surface shows the **no-op card itself** — *"697 extraction runs found nothing. That's a bug, not a quiet address book."* Not an empty state; an accusation, pointed at us.

### What the finding actually proves
I wrote *"ran 718× and found nothing"* as an **illustration of a rule**. It was live, at 697, on the owner's own instance.

1. **Honest-empty-state work isn't polish — it's instrumentation.** Every no-op card is a monitor a user reads for free. It should ship *with* each surface, not after.
2. **Watching's job list should show "wrote nothing" as a distinct outcome from "completed"** across every job, not just extraction. 697 completions were invisible only because completed-with-no-output looks identical to completed-successfully.
