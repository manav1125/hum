TAKEN FROM BEE · KEPT FROM US · THE UPGRADE RULES
Taken from Bee — the day as a story: cover headline in prose, the journey drawn, count orbs, ask-your-day chat, weekly patterns. The reason people open Bee daily is the journal payoff, not the task list. V1 gives us that payoff.
Kept from us (Bee has none of these) — the sync-lag pill on the cover · ○→✓ acceptance before anything files · provenance chips on every chat answer ("Dana · 10:31 · tap to open") · insights that are counts with receipts, never sentiment guesses · the bookmark as the loudest object · one-tap pause on every surface that shows "recording".
The location line: coarse place labels (HOME · VERVE · OFFICE) on a stylised arc — a memory aid, not a tracking map. No street map, no coordinates, and Places can be turned off in You → Devices → Halo without losing the rest of the Day.
Where each frame lands: V1/V2/V3 are the Day surface (cover → timeline → ask · This-week reached from the cover's date row) · V4 replaces H2's plain card whenever Halo is actively recording; H2's compact form remains the paused/idle/attention state · H1 onboarding, H4 detail and H5's ten states are unchanged underneath.
Motion budget: the breathing ring, the drifting journey dash, and the floating ⚑ — three live elements max per screen, all ≤3s loops, all frozen under Reduced Motion. Engagement comes from the content's warmth (serif headlines, aurora grounds, real quotes), not from things wiggling.
The one-line brief to code: the Day is the retention surface — it must feel like opening a journal someone wrote about you, and every beautiful thing on it must still pass the honesty rules. Beauty is the hook; the ✓ is the product.
