E5 · THE CONTRACT THAT MAKES IT FEEL NATIVE
One hero, everywhere — the sun path. Island minimal: a 12px arc stub. Today tile: the arc across the tile's top edge. Day cover: full sky. Recap: completed with the gradient of the whole day. Watch face later: the same arc. One drawing, five sizes — that's how the product becomes recognisable at a glance.
Shared-element transitions, not pushes. Today tile → Day: the tile's arc expands into the cover sky (spring, 420ms, damping .82). Chapter row → episode: the row's headline is the shared element; the scene header inflates behind it. Bead on the arc → episode: the bead grows into the header. Back always reverses the same path — nothing ever just appears.
The gesture grammar (three verbs only): swipe down anywhere on the Day = back to Today (cover deflates into the tile) · swipe left/right on the cover = previous/next day (sky cross-fades through the dates' own light) · long-press an episode = peek card with ✓/✕/share. No hidden fourth gesture; everything else is a visible tap.
Haptics map — `.light`: bead tap, chapter open · `.medium`: the dock catch, bookmark landing · `.success`: day-close "Good night" · `.error`: never for Halo (nothing here fails loudly; failures are quiet amber cards). The clip's own buzz remains the only confirmation the hardware ever gives.
Adopted from Bee (2026-08-30 review), honest form: the live strip — last-synced snippet in italics + segment timer + "as of 3 min ago"; the timer counts recorded segments, never claims a live mic · verb+destination chips on proposals ("▤ File to Renew Acme · ✉ Draft it for Dana") so accepting has a named outcome, with the dock animation unchanged · Key Takeaways block on episodes · summary templates via the ☰ chip · insight cards keep their verbs and gain "Ask about this ›". Rejected from Bee: street maps with pins, sentiment prose, acceptance-free filing, logo-of-Google branding on chips (we name our own destinations).
The connection tissue, named: every filed thing carries the same provenance pill — ◉ heard · 10:31 · Verve — in HQ, in the mission, in chat, on Dana's People page. Tapping it anywhere reopens the moment. That pill is the product's proof-of-magic and its audit trail — same object.
Premium is the experience, not a gate: nothing in these frames is a paywall. Halo owners get the Day; the frames never show a locked feature — the purchase already happened. What they're paying for is that this surface exists and is this good.
Honesty spine, unchanged and load-bearing: the lag pill lives on every sky · sky gradients come from the clock, never from inferred mood · beads = real episodes only · the dock only animates what a ✓ actually did · "audio discarded ✓" is a claim the retention setting makes true. Supersedes: V2's V1 cover and V4 tile (E1's grammar wins); V2's V2/V3 chat + patterns stand; H1–H5 states and onboarding stand.
