The funnel rule: never cripple, always preview. Standalone Halo is a complete diary product — nothing above is a locked screen. The upgrade moment is contextual and honest: when a suggestion could have been done for you, the card shows a quiet third chip — ✦ Cue could draft this — tappable into one real preview (the drafted email, watermarked, unsendable). One tap of real magic beats any paywall screen; the preview is capped at 3/week so it stays a gift, not a nag.
Why someone wears it daily without Cue: the morning "your day, remembered" push · perfect recall in any argument ("search: what did the contractor actually quote") · the on-this-day card · open-loop nudges before relationships fade · spoken notes that never lose a thought. Why they convert: every day, a few cards quietly show what Cue would have already handled. The diary sells the assistant by contrast, not by gate.
Privacy spine (standalone-critical): names and transcripts stay on-phone · per-person capture exclusion by name (G1) · places = phone location + user labels, Halo has no GPS (G2) · search runs locally (G3) · reassign teaches, never rewrites (G4) · audio discarded after understanding, everywhere. These six sentences are the App Store review answer and the dinner-party answer — same words.
