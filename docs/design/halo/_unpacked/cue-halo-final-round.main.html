CLOSING RULINGS
Before-Halo backfill: onboarding offers calendar import so day one isn't the first day of history — past days render as outline-only covers (calendar facts, no verdicts, no quotes). Halo never writes a verdict about a day it didn't hear.
Android parity (R6): Island states map to an ongoing notification (hearing/marked/quiet, same glyphs) + a 1×1 sun-path widget. The settler binds to the Assistant long-press where available. No feature is iOS-gated except the watch face.
Gap grammar, unified: three visually distinct absences — not worn (dim arc + caption), off the record (dashed span, chosen), forgotten (blank, permanent). Never interchangeable, never euphemized.
Button grammar, unified (fixes F5↔S2 conflict): click = ⚑ mark · double-click = ✦ note · hold 1s = record on/off (single buzz) · hold 3s = off the record (double buzz + LED dark — keep holding through the 1s stop, which previews as the first beat). One button, four verbs, each with a distinct haptic; F5's map updates to match.
Relive audio rule (S1): the "hear the 12 seconds" chip appears only while the clip still exists on-device pre-sync; after sync it's gone and the page shows the quote alone. No exception, including for delight.

S6 · FOUR RULINGS FROM CODE'S GAP LIST
1 · The verdict on an unremarkable day — writing rules. The verdict is an observation, never a grade. Four registers, chosen by day shape: outcome day → name the outcome ("The morning found Acme its number") · texture day (chapters, no outcomes) → name the texture, specifically ("Mostly errands, and a long call with Ma") · thin day (≤2 chapters) → name the one true thing, small is fine ("A quiet Tuesday — one good idea on the walk home") · gap day (>50% unheard) → scope first, then the fragment ("Of the afternoon I heard: the sprint got its cut"). Banned: scoring words (productive, slow, light), apology, and any sentence that could apply to every day. If no specific true sentence exists, the fallback is inventory, not poetry: "4 conversations, nothing that needed keeping." The verdict may be plain; it may never be generic.
2 · Beads: target 5–9 chapters on a normal day, hard cap 12. Your 3-min silence / 60-sec merge-forward floors are blessed as defaults, but tune to the count, not the constants: if a day exceeds 12, raise the silence threshold for that day until it fits (re-chaptering is per-day, invisible, and never splits a ⚑). Below 3 chapters, never pad — a two-bead day is a two-bead day. Beads under 5 minutes render at 70% size so density reads without crowding.
3 · "Draft it" opens the draft — it never files a ticket. ✓ on a draft-verb chip runs the agent immediately and opens the composer sheet with the draft in it (recipient, subject, body — editable). Send is yours; "Not now" parks the draft on the item with a ✎ badge. The dock animation fires on send or park, not on ✓ — the thing that docks is the real artifact. Rationale: the funnel's whole promise is "done for you, shown to you" — a work item that drafts later is neither. Offline or slow (>4s): the chip becomes "drafting…" with the item parked; the sheet arrives as a notification when ready.
4 · The unfilled arc is dotted; a gap is dim-solid. Three arc treatments, never interchangeable: lived = solid warm stroke · not yet = fine dotted line at 18% white (the R1 hour-one frame already draws this — "the rest of today is still unwritten") · gap = dim solid at 14% with its caption. Dotted reads as promise, dim-solid reads as record. The now-point carries a soft glow head so "filled to now" has an obvious tip, and at 8am a mostly-dotted arc reads as morning, not breakage.
