Halo on mobile · onboarding, the card, the Day · design answer

Always a little behind the room —
and honest about it.

The hardware truth that shapes every frame: Halo records, syncs in ~20-second segments, and is never live. So the design's one recurring gesture is the lag number, worn proudly — "12 min behind" on the card, on the Day, in the Live Activity. Nothing anywhere implies live listening; the three silent questions (is it on · what did it do · can I stop it) each get a permanent home.

Frames: H1 onboarding's three load-bearing steps · H2 the card + Live Activity · H3 the Day · H4 a capture · H5 the ten states · rulings at the end. Phase note per frame: everything except onboarding steps 1/3/4 ships identically in Phase A.

CARD RULES
One component, two homes (decision 1: both). Today shows it only while paired-and-active or in a state needing attention; You → Devices always shows it. Same markup, a `context` prop.
The sync line has exactly three shapes: "up to date" · "synced to N min ago" (moving) · "N min behind · out of range — still recording" (reassurance form). Silence is never one of the shapes.
Pause is stateful and loud: while paused the card goes slate (no red anywhere), reads "Paused by you · resume when you're ready" — unmistakable, and impossible to leave by accident because resume is the same single button.
Battery speaks early: at 20% the card adds "charge tonight" — before it matters, per the brief's forgotten-states table.

THE FOUR DAY QUESTIONS, RULED
Empty hours — a dashed hairline with the hour range and a stated reason. Never removed from the rail, never a spinner, never bare. The end-of-day quiet card says the state in words: "on and 3 min behind."
The bookmark is the loudest thing on the surface — amber ⚑ chip, amber border, "YOU MARKED THIS", and the quote is your words verbatim, not Cue's summary. It's the only human signal in the stream, so nothing Cue inferred may outrank it visually.
A proposal reads as a proposal — the ○ PROPOSED chip on a blue-tinted inset, ✓ accepts (files it, chip becomes the filed provenance pill), ✕ dismisses (one gesture, undo toast, and it teaches). Nothing from Halo lands in HQ un-accepted while the default mode is proposes.
Wrong ones — ✕ on the proposal, swipe-archive on the episode; both reuse existing gestures (`swipe-archive-row`, `undo-toast`). Dismissals feed the same sender/pattern learning as the valve's ✕.
Decision 2 · a new surface, not a Today mode. A day has a clock-shape Today doesn't; forcing episodes into Today's lane grammar loses the silence, and the silence is half the honesty. Reached from the card ("Open the Day ›") and swipeable between dates. It feeds Today — accepted proposals appear there like any other work.

H5 · The ten states — each a card shape, none a toast
NEVER PAIRED
"Have a Halo? Set it up in two minutes." — the setup door on the same card slot, dashed border, never an error tint.
OUT OF RANGE
"Away from your phone — still recording. Syncs when you're back." Blue info tint. The buffer is a feature; the copy says so.
SYNC BEHIND
"38 min behind · catching up" + a moving progress hairline. The number must visibly shrink — movement is the reassurance.
BONDED ELSEWHERE
"This Halo is linked to another phone." Recovery = red button + device name typed + "erases what it hasn't sent." Never a default style.
BATTERY LOW
At 20%, on the card: "~2h left — charge tonight." At 5% a push (counts against the budget). Never first heard at 2%.
STORAGE FULL
"Card is full — oldest already-synced audio is removed first. Nothing unsynced is touched." States the deletion rule before it runs.
PAUSED BY YOU
Whole card goes slate, no red anywhere: "Paused by you." Resume = the same single button. Cannot be left by accident — pausing never expires silently.
BLUETOOTH DENIED
Full screen, not a toast: "iOS asks once. Settings → Cue → Bluetooth, then come back." Deep-link button + the manual path in words.
TRANSCRIPTION UNAVAILABLE
"Your audio is safe on the phone — understanding is delayed." The couldn't-read grammar: a fact about the query, never rendered as an empty day.
NOTHING WORTH SURFACING
"A quiet one — worn 9h, nothing needed filing." Calm ◌ glyph, green-tinted label. A product position: quiet is success, not failure.
THE FIVE DECISIONS OWED
1 · Card lives in both — one component, `context` prop. Today shows it while active or needing attention; You → Devices always. Agreed with the recommendation.
2 · The Day is a new surface, reached from the card and swipeable between dates. A day has a clock-shape Today doesn't, and compressing silence is half the honesty. It feeds Today; it doesn't replace it. No fifth tab.
3 · Parked-and-propose default. Matches screen observation and the valve's fail-open philosophy: the wearer's trust is earned by the ○→✓ loop, not assumed. "Acts on the obvious" is offered at step 6 and honoured per-capture with the same provenance pills.
4 · Transcribe-and-discard default. Matches the frame pipeline. The capture detail says "audio discarded per your retention setting" so delete never over-promises. Keeping audio is an explicit switch in You → Devices → Halo, off by default.
5 · Privacy indicator: in-app the recording state is always visible (card pulse + Live Activity + Island dot). The device's own light is hardware's call — but the phone never claims more privacy than the device delivers, which is why every recording state uses the reserved red and the word "recording", never a euphemism.
Phase A honesty, placed: the cloud-transit fact lives on the promise screen (step 2) and in You → Devices → Halo → "How audio travels", not buried in settings. When B ships, the line disappears and the changelog says why that's better.
Inherited invariants: never fake a number (the lag is real or absent, never "up to date" as a guess) · nothing files without acceptance while in proposes mode · ✕ teaches · reserved red `#E5675B` = recording only · amber = the bookmark and destructive warnings · push budget shared — Halo battery/sync alerts replace, never double.
