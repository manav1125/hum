# v24 — mobile final: Library's two doors + the remaining surfaces (2026-08-03)

## Library gets two doors (frame F1)
Your call, with a mechanism rather than just a second link: **a sheet that rises over whatever you're doing.**

- **Door 1 — destination.** In the ⓶ menu, beside People.
- **Door 2 — sheet.** From any artefact strip, any "Made for this" section, or the composer's ▦.

**Opened contextually it leads with the current thing's files** ("From Renew Acme · 4") then everything else — so "where's that file" never costs you your place. Swipe down to return; nothing lost.

### The sheet rule
- A surface earns a sheet when **you need it without leaving what you're doing.** Library qualifies; "where's that file" happens mid-task constantly.
- **Sheets open contextually and say so** — from a thing, that thing's files first; from the composer, most recent first.
- **People gets no sheet** — you browse it deliberately and a person needs the full screen.
- **Config never gets a sheet** — changing a setting mid-task is the interruption we're avoiding.
