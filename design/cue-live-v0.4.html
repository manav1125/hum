The four modes · escalating power + trust

One presence, four levels of involvement

all governed by the trust/consent console · capture posture shown per mode

Companion
Follows your cursor, passive until summoned. Reads what you hover, offers the next move. AX only · most private
Scoped watch
Point Cue at one window/app. It captures action items & todos from just that. bounded capture
Always-on
Whole screen, continuous. Max power, opt-in only, visible light + one-tap pause. AX + vision on change
Take control
State a goal, Cue drives. Guided (it points, you click) → autonomous (it acts, you approve). checkpointed

It all flows to one place

Desktop capture → memory → next moves

Same spine as everything else
What Cue Live sees you do, decide, and promise on screen writes into the 8-type memory and the unified next-moves queue — exactly like meeting capture and the wearable. Desktop is the digital sibling of the pin.
What we reuse vs. build
Reuse: vellum's computer-use, MCP, approvals, CES, memory + the native mac helper. Borrow: clicky's overlay/point UX, a macos-use-style MCP for actions, screenpipe-style local capture. Build: the AX reader, the overlay HUD, the mode/consent layer.
Positioning vs. the funded field (Cluely, Highlight): they read your screen but forget you. Cue Live is backed by persistent memory and one cross-surface identity — and it's self-hosted and AX-first, so it's private by construction.
