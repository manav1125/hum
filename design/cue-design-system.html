One assistant.
Every surface. Nothing dropped.

Desktop, web, and mobile share one memory and one identity. Start a thought on your Mac, finish it by voice on your phone, and the action items from your last meeting are already waiting — captured, organized, and ready to act on.

Design language

Calm, sharp, decisive

Slate ink and an electric-blue accent. Green means success, never brand. Generous whitespace, soft 12–16px corners, one quiet hairline. DM Sans throughout, DM Mono for the machine voice — timestamps, sources, states.
