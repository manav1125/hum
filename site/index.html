FAQ

The things people ask.

Is my data used to train AI models?▾
No — never. Your data is never sold and never used to train models. Cue is a fully managed, full-stack solution: nothing to host and no keys to bring. Your memory and data stay yours, encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export or delete everything at any time.
Which AI models does it use?▾
Cue routes every task to the best model for it — matching the query to the right frontier model automatically. You don't pick or manage models; Cue routes to the strongest one for the job and keeps every run inside the guardrails you set.
Does it run on my machine?▾
Yes — the core runs as a local daemon. Nothing passes through our servers. The UI is a local webapp; Cue Live runs natively on macOS. Self-hosted and cloud-hosted options both available.
What can't it do?▾
It can't access accounts you haven't connected, can't use tools you've set to "never," and won't take any high-impact action without your explicit approval. It also won't operate on systems it hasn't been granted permission to touch.
How is Cue different from ChatGPT or Claude?▾
ChatGPT and Claude are chat interfaces — you ask, they answer, they forget. Cue is a persistent agent: it remembers across sessions, acts proactively without being asked, and actually does things (sends email, books tables, files notes) — not just answers questions about them.
Is it available now?▾
In early access for design partners. Join the waitlist below and we'll reach out — priority to founders, operators, and people with genuinely too much on their plates.
